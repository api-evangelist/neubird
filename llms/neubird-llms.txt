# NeuBird AI

NeuBird AI is The Production Operations Agent platform that autonomously investigates, triages, and resolves
production incidents. It connects to your existing observability and operations stack — Datadog,
Splunk, CloudWatch, PagerDuty, ServiceNow, Slack, Kubernetes, and 30+ other tools — and delivers
evidence-backed root cause analysis in under 3 minutes, without requiring human investigation for
L1 and L2 incidents. Used by SRE, DevOps, and Platform Engineering teams at enterprises running
AWS, Azure, and OpenShift environments.

Key metrics: 87% MTTR reduction, 90% alert noise suppression, <3 min average resolution time.
